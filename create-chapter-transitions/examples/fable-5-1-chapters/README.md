# Fable 5.1 chapter cards

Editable HyperFrames source for the ten 1.6-second chapter cards inserted into the Borumi project `Fable 5.1` (`5689`). The cards use the reusable grid-and-typed-title treatment from the parent `create-chapter-transitions` skill.

`rows.json` contains the rendered titles. `edit-plan.json` records the transcript-aligned Borumi insertion points and the scene-two surgeon B-roll placement.

The visual card is intentionally silent. The Borumi timeline owns the separate restrained typing cue so its level and timing remain editable.

The ten rendered MP4s are 1920×1080, H.264, 30 fps, and 1.6 seconds each. The finished Borumi edits were committed as `0304` (Scene 2 surgeon B-roll and credit) and `53b5` (chapter cards and sound cues).
