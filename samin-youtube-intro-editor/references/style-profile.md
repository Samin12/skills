# Samin YouTube Intro Style Profile

Derived from recent Samin Yasar YouTube intros reviewed in July 2026. Treat this as a durable baseline, then follow the current video's topic and supplied references.

## Narrative pattern

- Open on proof, not biography or setup. Finished sites, generated outputs, dashboards, and before/after results appear during the first sentence.
- Tease the complete outcome before teaching the process.
- Organize long explanations into numbered levels, paths, steps, or use cases.
- Alternate between real interfaces and simplified visual explanations.
- Return to full talking head briefly to reset attention before a new section.

## Frame system

- YouTube landscape, usually 16:9.
- Warm white canvas with a subtle gray grid is the most recognizable explainer background.
- Headlines are black, heavy, often slightly condensed or italic. Section numerals use a saturated blue or purple circle/pill.
- Presenter sits bottom-right in a rounded rectangle, generally 22–27% of canvas width and 25–32% of canvas height.
- Product or site media occupies the remaining 65–80% of the frame and is frequently enlarged beyond web-safe scale.
- Comparisons use asymmetric split frames, not equal card grids.

## Motion and pacing

- Rapid proof montage at the hook, followed by longer tutorial holds.
- Hard cuts and quick spatial pushes dominate; dissolves are reserved for continuity.
- Screenshots are cropped, zoomed, scrolled, tilted, or paired with an oversized cursor. Raw static screenshots are avoided.
- Numbered step cards build in layers: number first, label second, evidence third.
- Camera PiP remains stable enough to anchor the eye while the surrounding world moves.
- Motion peaks on the emphasized noun or result, not on every word.

## Visual hierarchy

1. The claim or proof.
2. The product/site/workflow being discussed.
3. Presenter PiP.
4. Supporting metadata, connectors, and decoration.

Use no more than one short headline and one supporting label at once. The spoken narration remains primary.

## Recommended defaults

- Background: `#F7F5F0` or a nearby warm neutral.
- Ink: `#101114`.
- Accent: choose one of `#2F64F5`, `#4F46E5`, or topic-specific supplied brand color.
- Secondary highlight: occasional hot pink or coral only when the source/reference uses it.
- Grid: 40–56 px cells at 7–12% contrast.
- PiP: 24 px radius, 3–5 px light outline, soft shadow.
- Headline: 72–116 px; section labels 28–42 px; production metadata 18–24 px.

## Avoid

- A supplied-image slideshow with no conceptual transformation.
- Small browser screenshots floating in unused space.
- Generic cyan/purple AI gradients.
- Repeating identical cards for every point.
- Captions competing with large editorial text unless captions were requested.
- Keeping every scene animated at the same speed.
- Using real small creators as “bad” examples. Make the bad example fictional or generic.
