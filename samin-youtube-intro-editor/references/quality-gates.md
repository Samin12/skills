# Quality Gates

## Editorial

- The first five seconds state and show the core promise.
- The first 20 seconds contain multiple distinct pieces of proof.
- Every major spoken promise has a matching visual.
- Negative examples are generic or fictional; positive examples are relevant and legible.
- The visual pace slows after the hook without becoming static.

## Presenter PiP

- Bottom-right placement is consistent except for deliberate full-screen resets.
- Face and hands are not awkwardly cropped.
- The PiP never covers essential website controls or labels.
- Existing screen-share footage is promoted full-frame rather than shrunk into an unreadable PiP.

## Motion

- Every screenshot has an intentional treatment: crop, pan, zoom, scroll, tilt, cursor, or depth.
- Entrances vary in direction, duration, and easing.
- Motion resolves before the audience needs to read.
- No continuous ambient motion is driven by wall-clock time.
- No flash frames appear at clip boundaries.

## Technical

- Assets are local and load without network access.
- HyperFrames lint/check passes.
- Snapshot review covers the hook and every major story turn.
- Output video and audio durations differ by less than 100 ms unless intentional.
- Output has H.264 or another user-approved delivery codec, AAC audio, yuv420p, and 1920×1080 unless otherwise specified.
- Audio is intelligible and not clipped; original sync is preserved.
- The rendered file plays from beginning to end and has no black/blank frames.

## Delivery

- Report actual duration and resolution.
- Provide the MP4 and a snapshot/contact sheet.
- Preserve the source file and keep the editable project.
- Name any unresolved limitation plainly.
